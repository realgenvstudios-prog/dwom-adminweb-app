# PRIVACY POLICY

DWOM ("we", "us", "our") provides a platform for users to browse, purchase groceries and food products from verified merchants and have them delivered to your doorstep ("Services"). We are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data. For questions, contact us at support@dwom.app.

## 1. Changes to This Policy

We update this Privacy Policy as needed to comply with Ghana's Data Protection Act 2012 and other applicable laws. Changes will be published on our website and app.

## 2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information when you:
- Register for an account (name, phone, email, address)
- Browse or purchase products
- Subscribe to delivery services
- Use our app or website
- Contact customer support
- Rate products or provide feedback
- With your consent, from third parties like payment processors or delivery partners

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:
- Provide delivery services and process orders
- Match you with nearby merchants and delivery riders
- Send order updates and delivery tracking information
- Improve our services and customer experience
- Prevent fraud and illegal activities
- Comply with applicable laws
- Send marketing communications (if you opt-in)
- Respond to your questions or complaints

## 4. Your Consent

By using DWOM, you consent to our collection and processing of your personal information as described in this policy. You can withdraw consent or opt out of marketing communications at any time by contacting support@dwom.app or through your account settings.

## 5. The Personal Information We Collect

We collect only essential information to provide our Services:
- Account information: name, email, phone, address, date of birth
- Order history: products purchased, delivery addresses, preferences
- Payment information: processed securely through third-party providers
- Device information: OS type, unique identifiers, app version
- Location data: via GPS and IP address for delivery
- Delivery rider information: name, phone, vehicle details (for safety)
- Ratings and reviews you provide
- Subscription information and preferences

## 6. How We Collect Your Information

We collect information in three ways:
- **Directly from you**: When you create accounts, place orders, contact support, rate products, or subscribe to services
- **Automatically**: Through device sensors, location services, app usage, and browsing behavior
- **From partners**: Payment processors, delivery riders, and merchants (with your authorization)

## 7. Third-Party Services

Our platform may include third-party services (Facebook, WhatsApp, Twitter, etc.) that have their own privacy policies. We're not responsible for their data practices. We recommend reviewing their privacy statements before sharing information with them.

We're not liable for content or data practices on linked sites, though we work to maintain platform integrity.

## 8. Location Services

Location services must be enabled for us to identify nearby merchants and assign delivery riders. We request permission to access your location before first use.

We collect location data via:
- GPS coordinates
- IP address
- Wi-Fi and device sensors

You can change location permissions in your device settings at any time.

## 9. Communications

You can contact customer support, merchants, and delivery riders through calls or messages in our app. We record these communications (voice and text) for:
- Safety and quality assurance
- Fraud prevention
- Dispute resolution
- Service improvement

By using this feature, you consent to recording and use of these communications.

## 10. Surveys

We may request you to complete optional surveys to improve our services. Surveys may collect demographic information (age, profession, income) and usage feedback. By participating, you consent to our collection and use of this information.

## 11. Disclosures of Your Personal Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share your data with:
- Merchants and delivery partners needed to fulfill your orders
- Payment processors and financial institutions for transactions
- Fraud prevention and debt collection agencies
- Law enforcement and regulatory authorities when legally required
- Our investors, auditors, and service providers
- Third parties with your explicit consent

We ensure all third parties comply with data protection laws and limit their use of your information only to providing services to you.

## 12. Liability Disclaimer

DWOM manages order fulfillment and delivery. Merchants operate as listed on our platform, and delivery riders operate as independent contractors. We are not responsible for:
- Merchant representations or product fitness
- Rider actions beyond our explicit Terms of Use
- Order accuracy or product quality issues beyond our control

## 13. Storage and Security

We protect your information using physical, technical, and administrative security measures including:
- Encrypted databases in secure data centers
- SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
- Firewalls and access controls
- Limited employee access to personal information
- Regular security monitoring

Your information is backed up securely. We never ask for secure information through unsolicited contact. You are responsible for keeping your password and account details confidential. We are not liable for unauthorized access beyond our control.

## 14. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for:
- **7 years** after your last active use of our platform
- Shorter periods if you request deletion (subject to legal/investigation exceptions)
- Indefinitely if you're subject to ongoing investigations or disputes

We delete information immediately upon death verification by next of kin.

## 15. Marketing Communications

We may contact you with information about merchants, products, special offers, and promotions via email, SMS, and other digital channels if you subscribe to receive marketing communications.

**Opt Out:**
Click "Unsubscribe" in marketing emails or message support@dwom.app. We may still send non-promotional messages like order receipts, delivery updates, and account notifications.

## 16. External Links

Our website and app may contain links to third-party websites. We have no control over their content or data practices and are not responsible for them. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before sharing information.

## 17. Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access, correct, amend, or delete your personal information
- Request anonymization or blocking of unnecessary data
- Receive your data in a portable format
- Revoke your consent at any time
- Delete your account (subject to unresolved claims)

**How to Exercise Your Rights:**
Submit requests via email to support@dwom.app or through our Data Subject Request Form. Please provide identification for verification. We'll respond as soon as possible. 

We may retain your information if there's an unresolved issue with your account, legal requirements exist, or it's necessary for fraud prevention and security. We will redact third-party or confidential information where needed.
